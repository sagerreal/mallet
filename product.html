AI Front Desk

Every call answered. Every job booked.

Mallet picks up your business line day or night — qualifies the caller, checks the schedule, books the slot, and texts the confirmation. Emergencies get flagged, tire-kickers get handled, and you get the job instead of the shop down the road.

  • Answers calls and texts on your existing business number
  • Books directly into the real schedule — no double-booking
  • Knows your services, your service area, and your hours
  • Escalates true emergencies to a human instantly
